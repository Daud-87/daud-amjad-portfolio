Designed and built end to end.

What it does

  • Live weather (Open-Meteo) and an instant currency converter for trip budgeting.

Platform

  • Role-based dashboards for User, Travel Guide, and Admin.
  • Package browser (All / Air / Road) with filters, ratings, and cart.
  • AI Predictor — destination, budget, days, and transport in; ranked package matches out.
  • Admin panel for bookings, payments, package requests, and revenue analytics.
